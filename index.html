How a project goes

  1. Talk it through

    A short call or email about what you sell, who buys it, and what isn't working right now.

  2. Plan and quote

    A written plan with a fixed price and a timeline. No surprise invoices.

  3. Design and build

    You see real drafts early and give feedback before anything is final.

  4. Launch and hand off

    Everything is set up in your accounts, so you own it. You get the files and a short how-to.
